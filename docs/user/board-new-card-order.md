# Where a new card lands

A new card goes to the **bottom** of the column you created it in, under everything already there.
Make three cards in a row and the column reads top to bottom in the order you made them.

This holds however the card was made — the New card dialog, an agent creating one for you, or a
plan being split into child cards — and it holds whichever board you are looking at. Viewing all
projects at once merges every project's cards into one column per stage, so "the bottom" means the
bottom of what you can actually see, not the bottom of one project's share of it. Filtering down
to a single project does not move anything: a card under everything on the merged board is still
under everything on its own.

The one exception is Done, which reads newest-first — see [Where a finished card lands](./board-done-order.md).
A card created straight into Done goes to the top of it, exactly as a card that finishes there does.

## Moving it afterwards

Dragging a card puts it exactly where you dropped it. Position is yours to change once the card
exists; the bottom is only where it starts.
