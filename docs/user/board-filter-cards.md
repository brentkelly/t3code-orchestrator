# Filter the board to the cards you mean

The board's top bar has a **Filter cards** box beside the **New card** button. Type in it and every
column narrows to the cards whose title or key matches what you typed — "T3O-16" finds one card,
"merge" finds every card about merging. Case does not matter, and neither does where in the title
the match falls.

Filtering only changes what you can see. The cards you filtered out keep their stage and their
place in the column, agents keep working, and dragging a visible card still drops it where you put
it relative to the cards you cannot see.

To clear the filter, select the **✕** in the box or press **Escape** while typing in it. If nothing
matches, the board says so under the top bar rather than leaving you with eight empty columns.
