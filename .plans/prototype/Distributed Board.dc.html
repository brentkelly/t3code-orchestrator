T3Code / t3o · distributed board

Distributed board

One central server hosts the board and places cards on workers (other T3o machines). Screens are grouped by surface, left to right in flow order. Copy inside each mock is final. With no workers added, every surface renders exactly as today: no worker chip, no worker picker, no grouping in Concurrency, no Project checkouts page.

Status colours Blue · connecting, cloning, queued step running Violet · waiting on you (e.g. connection needs sign-in) Amber · offline, access revoked, incompatible, clone failed, card held Grey · online (steady), queued, paused by you Green · an action just finished (paired, cloned)
