Everything else sits in sections that start collapsed; each closed row still shows its counts, so you only open the one you care about. Click a tile or a row to open it.
Cost is in the overview, a fixed tile, so it can't be pushed out of view by long content. Not done items surface in the overview, since they're the ones you act on. Sections reset to closed on every card.
